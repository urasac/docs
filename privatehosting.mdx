# Private Server Hosting Guide

![Aaa](/images/aaa.png "Aaa")

Welcome to the definitive guide for managing your **Courtroom Private Server**! Since private servers are **100% free** and fully optimized, they serve as the perfect sandbox for hosting custom legal roleplay sessions, group events, or league matches with total control.

---

## Getting Started: Host Setup

When you first join your private server, the system automatically recognizes you as the server owner:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Automatic Admin Grant">
    You will automatically be granted full administrative permissions upon joining your server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Auto Team Assignment">
    You are automatically placed on the **Administration Team**. This team holds high-level privileges, including full access to restricted doors, specialized seats, and utility functions across the courthouse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Best Practice for Hosts">
    If you are purely managing or hosting the event and **not actively participating** in the trial roleplay, we strongly recommend remaining on the **Administration Team** for optimal control over the environment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Essential Admin Commands

Your private server comes packed with a wide variety of administrative tools to manage trial flow and enforce order.

### Command Access & Reference

To view the complete, up-to-date list of available commands directly in-game, run any of the following:

- `:commands`
- `:cmds`

_You can also open the default Roblox command bar to view and execute commands._

---

### Core Hosting Commands

Here are the most critical commands every host should know:

| Command | Usage | Description |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `/announce` | `/announce Text here!` | Displays a prominent server-wide announcement banner to all active players. |
| `/barrier` | `/barrier` | Toggles and locks the physical court barrier, creating a wall that prevents spectators in the gallery from intruding into the trial area. |

---

## Safety, Moderation & Audit Logs

To maintain a secure and safe environment for all players, our system tracks server activity behind the scenes.

<Warning>
  Every single administrative action, command usage, and moderation event in private servers is logged in real-time.

  Any intentional misuse, command abuse, or violation of global Roblox & Paraside Group standards will lead to strict consequences, including permanent revocation of private server hosting privileges and potential account moderation.
</Warning>
