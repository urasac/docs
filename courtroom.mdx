# Welcome to Courtroom 🏛️

![Defend](/images/defend.png "Defend")

First of all, **thank you for playing and supporting us!**

**Courtroom** is our flagship interactive roleplay experience on Roblox designed to deliver a realistic, immersive, and engaging judicial system simulation. Whether you want to deliver justice as a Judge, defend a client as an Attorney, or maintain order as a Deputy, Courtroom provides all the tools, environments, and features you need to create compelling legal stories.
